## Hook and structure (first 1–3 seconds)
- Open on the **finished result or the most surprising shot**, not a logo or a title card.
- Put the hook as on-screen text in the first frame, e.g. "$689K, 4 bed, 2-car garage".
- Structure for a 30 s listing video:
  - 0–3 s: hook (exterior or the best room, with price on screen)
  - 3–20 s: 3 to 4 rooms, one short beat each (2–4 s per shot)
  - 20–27 s: the single best feature (kitchen, yard, view)
  - 27–30 s: CTA on screen ("Comment TOUR" or "Link in bio")
- Voiceover: 2.5 words per second is a practical pace [U]. A 30 s script is about 70–80 words.

## Captions and keywords
- Caption: 1–2 sentences, with the city and property type in the first line. TikTok search reads the caption.
- Use 3–5 hashtags: one location, one property type, one niche.
- Subtitles: burn captions in (CapCut or Submagic). Auto-captions improve retention on silent viewing.

## Do
- Vertical 9:16 full-frame. Keep the key subject away from the bottom 20% (UI overlay) [U].
- Trending audio is optional; for listing content, a clean royalty-free track often outperforms it.
- Show a person: a shot of the agent walking in, or a hand on a door, increases watch time [U].

## Don't
- Don't post a silent 30 s slideshow of stills with no hook.
- Don't put a phone number or address on screen (MLS and privacy rules; see mls-listing-rules.md).
- Don't use stock "dream home" music that a viewer hears on every real-estate video.
