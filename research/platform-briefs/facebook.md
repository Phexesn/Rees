# Facebook: Long-Form Posts, Marketplace and Groups

As of 2026-11-03. Meta help pages were not reachable; policy items below come from broker and industry reporting and must be re-checked.

Tags: **[S]** = secondary source (search snippet). **[U]** = unverified, commonly cited.

## Long-form post structure (for prompts)
1. **Opening line:** the property and the single strongest reason to look (price drop, school, new roof).
2. **2–4 short paragraphs:** features, neighborhood, a fact that is verifiable (commute time, school rating with source).
3. **Clear CTA:** "Message me for a showing" or "Reply 'INFO'". Facebook links are shown as previews, so a link is acceptable.
4. Include the brokerage name and license line if state rules require it.

## Groups
- Read each group's rules. Many local groups ban listing posts entirely; post only in groups that allow it, and at the allowed frequency.
- Lead with value (market data, neighborhood info), not a sale pitch. Groups penalize pure ads.

## Marketplace
- Listing copy: lead with price and address area, not the exact address.
- Use the brokerage name in the description.
- Do not auto-post the same copy to Marketplace and Groups in one burst; spam signals may limit reach or lock the account [U].

## Don't
- No posts that could read as a "blind ad" (no brokerage name, no license).
- No Fair Housing risk language (see mls-listing-rules.md).
- No copy-paste of MLS remarks; reformat for the audience.
