## Feed caption structure (use for prompts)
1. **Line 1 (hook, under ~125 chars):** price or a concrete detail. "Just listed: 4BR in Oak Park with a 2-car garage for $689K" beats "Dream home alert!"
2. **Body (2–5 short lines):** 3 features, neighborhood fact, one reason to call. Line breaks, not paragraphs.
3. **CTA:** one action ("DM 'TOUR' for times" or "Link in bio"). Instagram does not make links clickable in captions.
4. **Hashtags:** 3–5, at the end, mixing location (#AustinRealEstate) and niche (#FirstTimeHomeBuyer). Do not use 30-tag blocks.
5. **Alt text** on every image: describe the room, not the marketing. "Kitchen with white shaker cabinets and a gas range" not "Stunning kitchen!!"

## Reels captions
- Caption is secondary to on-screen text and audio. Keep the caption under ~150 characters for the visible part. Put keywords in the caption because search indexes it.
- Use 3–5 hashtags. Stop using hashtag stacks.

## Do
- Post the exact list price and city if the MLS permits it (check MLS and broker advertising rules; see mls-listing-rules.md).
- Use one clear CTA per post.
- Write in the agent's voice, with first-person verbs ("I walked this home today").

## Don't
- No phone numbers or links in captions; they are not clickable. Use "link in bio".
- No Fair Housing risk words (see mls-listing-rules.md).
- No 30-hashtag blocks; the 5-tag cap is enforced.
- No "Tag a friend who..." engagement bait. Not a verified policy item, but it reads as spam.
