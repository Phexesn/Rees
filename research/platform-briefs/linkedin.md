## Angle A: Relocation (for corporate or transferring buyers)
- Audience: HR leads, relocation managers, and employees on transfer.
- Lead with the relocation problem solved: "Relocating to Austin in 30 days? Here's what our last 12 corporate transfers asked first."
- Include verifiable local data, cited (school district, commute, tax rate from an official source).
- Offer a concrete service: relocation packet, virtual tour, or a 20-minute call.

## Angle B: Investment (for investor and rental-property content)
- Audience: investors, financial advisors, small business owners.
- Lead with numbers: cap rate, gross yield, days on market (cite source and date).
- State assumptions. Investment claims need disclaimers; keep it factual, no guaranteed returns.
- Avoid "guaranteed", "can't lose", or appreciation predictions without a source.

## Structure
1. Hook line (the number or the problem).
2. 3–5 short paragraphs or bullets with data.
3. One CTA: "DM me for the comp sheet" or "Comment your market and I'll send data".
4. 3–5 hashtags.

## Do
- Post the agent's own experience and named local market data.
- Use the brokerage and license number in the profile, not every post.

## Don't
- Don't post MLS listings verbatim (MLS usage terms apply).
- Don't include Fair Housing risk language; many LinkedIn readers are on corporate HR teams that screen for it.
- Don't use fixed-return or "passive income guaranteed" language.
