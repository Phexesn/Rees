## Format for listing posts
- Free account: aim for 180–240 characters including link. Structure: price and city, one feature, link, one hashtag or none.
- Premium: a thread of 3–4 posts works for a property tour. Put the photo on post 1, the link on the last post.
- Use the link to the listing page, not a bare MLS number.

## Language-specific note for the prompt writers
- Chinese, Japanese, and Korean copy effectively has half the length budget. Generate CJK posts with a separate counter, and do not reuse English length limits.

## Do
- Lead with the price or the number.
- Use one image with a clear alt text.

## Don't
- No more than 2 hashtags; X hashtag use is low-engagement.
- No link shorteners that trigger spam filters.
- No Fair Housing risk wording.
