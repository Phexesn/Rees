## Structure for a 30–45 s listing Short
- 0–2 s: hook text on screen, with price or the best feature.
- 2–35 s: 3–5 quick shots with a voiceover or captions.
- Last 3 s: "Full tour on YouTube" or "Link in description" (describe, don't show the URL).

## Titles and descriptions
- Title: "[Beds] bed [type] in [city] | $[price]" format works well for search [U].
- Description: 1–2 lines, the city and address area (not exact address), 3 hashtags.
- Add the full walkthrough as a long-form video and link from the Short's description; Shorts drive discovery, not conversion.

## Do
- Keep text large and high-contrast in the top third (YouTube overlays the bottom area).
- Reuse the same master vertical file for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, with platform-specific captions.

## Don't
- Don't upload horizontal video cropped to vertical with black bars.
- Don't rely on Shorts for lead capture; viewers do not click through much.
- Don't put contact info in a Shorts description where it violates MLS rules (see mls-listing-rules.md, public remarks rules apply to marketing copy by broker policy).
