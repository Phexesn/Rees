## 1. All-in-one / CRM

### kvCORE / BoldTrail (Inside Real Estate)
- **Features:** CRM, IDX websites, lead gen, brokerage tools. Renamed BoldTrail per vendor comparison pages.
- **Pricing:** Not public. Third-party: solo about $299–$500/mo; teams about $499 (up to 3 users) to $749 (5 users). Brokerage-affiliated discounts reported (one user cites $50/mo as an eXp agent).
- **Strengths:** Broad all-in-one. G2 BoldTrail page: 4.5/5 across 1,535 reviews (per search snippet).
- **Weaknesses:** Steep learning curve, expensive, limited mobile app (per reviews).
- **Review complaints:** "Customer Service is wholly non-responsive and ineffective" (TrustRadius, 2022). "kvCORE - FAILS in my opinion" (TrustRadius, 2021). r/realtors (Dec 2023): a team moved Chime to kvCORE, "hated it," and went back.
- **Threat:** High.
- **Sources:** https://www.luxurypresence.com/?p=43348 · https://www.g2.com/products/boldtrail/reviews · https://www.trustradius.com/reviews/kvcore-2022-04-13-06-32-43 · https://www.trustradius.com/reviews/kvcore-2021-10-05-10-05-49 · https://www.selecthub.com/real-estate-crm-software/kvcore-vs-follow-up-boss/

### Lofty (formerly Chime)
- **Features:** CRM, IDX websites, automation ("Smart Plan"), e-signature (G2 comparison says it scores well), AI tools (described as early-stage in a Capterra review).
- **Pricing:** Not public. Seven plans listed as of Aug 2026 (per search snippet). Aggregator: median contract $12,500/yr from 10 buyer reports (directional only).
- **Strengths:** All-in-one, mobile app, search and websites praised in Capterra reviews.
- **Weaknesses:** Pricing complexity; support responsiveness.
- **Ratings:** G2 about 4.3 (399) to 4.4 (384), counts vary by page. Capterra 3.9 (125). Some 2026 reviews are vendor-invited.
- **Review complaints:** Pricing complexity, support. Capterra reviewer: mass emails reach only the primary contact on merged records.
- **Threat:** High.
- **Sources:** https://www.g2.com/products/lofty-lofty/pricing · https://www.capterra.com/p/157457/Lofty/reviews?page=2 · https://www.costbench.com/software/real-estate-crm/lofty-crm/

### Follow Up Boss
- **Features:** CRM, lead routing from 200+ lead providers, smart lists, automated follow-up, open API.
- **Pricing:** Grow $69/user/mo; Pro $499/mo; Platform $1,000/mo (up to 30 users) per aggregator. Free trial (14 days). Prospeo estimates real cost with calling about $108/user.
- **Strengths:** Strong for teams with lead volume. Capterra ease-of-use 4.5 (57 reviews).
- **Weaknesses:** Steep jump in price between tiers; no in-app transaction management (per one review); IDX, social and SEO need separate vendors (per one review).
- **Review complaints:** Capterra 2020 review: "difficult to learn and use." Hard to navigate with large teams. Smart list and template setup. Tagging and alerts sometimes inconsistent (Capterra overview).
- **Threat:** High.
- **Sources:** https://www.capterra.com/p/130020/Follow-Up-Boss/reviews/ · https://www.capterra.com/p/130020/Follow-Up-Boss/pricing/ · https://prospeo.io/s/follow-up-boss-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons · https://tomba.io/blog/follow-up-boss-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons

### Wise Agent
- **Features:** CRM, transaction management, texting add-on (WiseText), custom website add-on.
- **Pricing:** About $49/mo base (several sources), covers up to 5 team members on a shared login. One tracker: $42/mo annual ($499/yr). WiseText SMS $11/mo plus $80 setup. Custom website from $225/mo (tracker). 14-day trial.
- **Strengths:** Low entry price. Capterra 4.4 (531 reviews). Reviewers praise support.
- **Weaknesses:** Add-ons raise total cost. Dated look (not verified). G2 3.6 (15 reviews) cited by one tracker.
- **Threat:** High. Closest price band to RealtyBoost's $29 and $79 tiers.
- **Sources:** https://www.capterra.com/p/20680/The-Wise-Agent/pricing/ · https://costbench.com/software/real-estate-crm/wise-agent/ · https://costbench.com/software/real-estate-crm/wise-agent/hidden-costs/ (add-on figures, likely source; mapping inferred) · https://prospeo.io/s/wise-agent-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons

### Rechat
- **Features:** All-in-one CRM and marketing; agent websites and marketing templates; marketed as AI-powered (vendor).
- **Pricing:** Not public (G2 and a 2026 guide). Conflicting third-party figures: $49/mo (ToolRadar), $59/mo agent tier, "starting at $100" (Capterra). Unverified.
- **Strengths:** G2 4.6 (58 reviews); ToolRadar 4.7 (51). Reviewer: "all-in-one solution that delivers real value."
- **Weaknesses:** Deployment needs configuration and change management. Review dislikes include agent websites and marketing templates.
- **Threat:** High.
- **Sources:** https://www.g2.com/products/rechat/pricing · https://www.capterra.com/p/191234/Rechat/ · https://toolradar.com/compare/rechat-vs-wise-agent

## Takeaways for positioning

1. Against the all-in-ones (kvCORE, Lofty, FUB, Rechat), lead with a **published price list** and **simple onboarding**. Repeated complaints are about quote-only pricing, steep learning curves and support that fades after onboarding.
2. Against the copy tools (ListingAI, ListingCopy.ai), the gap is **bundling**: copy plus CRM plus microsite plus five languages in one login.
3. Against Wise Agent and LionDesk at the $29 band, compete on **language support and older-agent UX**. Research found no vendor with a multilingual UI, so that is the clearest open space. Verify before claiming it.
4. Treat transaction tools, showing tools and eSign as **integrate, don't build**.
5. Several prices above conflict across sources. Before publishing any comparison, get vendor pricing pages in hand (the session could not reach them).
