# Template - Open House Sign-In

**In short:** A sign-in sheet that asks only what you need and records consent clearly. The consent language below is an **example: have your broker approve it** (consent laws vary and attorney review is recommended). See [[Texting & Calling Leads (TCPA)]], [[Email Marketing Rules (CAN-SPAM)]], [[Open House Playbook]] and [[Open House Follow-Up]].

## Header
**Open House: [address]** | Date: ____ | Hosted by: [Agent name], [Brokerage], License No. ____

Signing in is optional. You are welcome to tour without signing in.

## Columns (one row per visitor group)
| Name | Phone | Email | Working with an agent? (Y/N) | Interested in similar homes? (Y/N) | Contact OK? (check) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

## Optional consent checkbox (example; **have your broker approve**)
- [ ] I agree that [Agent name] and [Brokerage] may contact me by phone call, text message and email about this property, and about real estate services. I understand that messages may be sent using an automated system, that message and data rates may apply, that consent is not required to purchase any property or service, and that I can opt out at any time by replying STOP or by telling the agent. 
Signature/initials: ____ Date: ____

## Notes for the host
- [ ] Do not make the checkbox pre-checked.
- [ ] Do not require sign-in to enter unless the seller or building requires it for security (check with broker).
- [ ] Tell visitors how you will use their information.
- [ ] Keep sheets private and secure; do not leave earlier visitors' details visible.
- [ ] Use a copy of the sheet for each event.
- [ ] Record which visitors consented and which did not in your CRM.
- [ ] Follow up as in [[Open House Follow-Up]].

## Fair housing
Treat every visitor the same. Do not comment on neighbors or who lives nearby. See [[Fair Housing Advertising]].
