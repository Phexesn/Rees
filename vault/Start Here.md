# 👋 Start Here

## How to use this playbook

1. Open [[Home]] — it lists every note by topic.
2. **Use search** (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + F in Obsidian) for anything: "open house", "verticals", "buyer agreement".
3. Blue links like [[Fair Housing Advertising]] jump to related notes.
4. **Templates** (in `06 Templates`) are meant to be copied. Duplicate one before you fill it in.
5. Checklists `- [ ]` can be ticked right inside Obsidian.

## How to read the labels

Every note starts with a small info block:

- `last_verified` — the date the facts were last checked.
- `status: verified` — checked against the sources listed at the bottom.
- `status: partly-verified` — some facts come from search summaries or secondary sources, and are marked **(unverified)** or **(verify)** in the text. Double-check those before relying on them.
- **(common practice)** — standard professional practice rather than a rule or a statistic.

Every factual note ends with a **Sources** list so you can check things yourself.

## Important

- **Not legal advice.** Real estate rules change and differ by state, MLS and brokerage. When a note says "ask your broker", please do.
- **Scripts are examples.** Make them sound like you. Never pressure anyone.
- **Fair Housing first.** Describe the property, never the people who should live there. See [[Fair Housing Advertising]].
- **Prices, platform limits and app menus change often.** Check the date on each note.
