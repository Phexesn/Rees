# Where to Follow the News

**In short:** Pick two or three trade outlets, one data source, and one podcast, and check them weekly. Trade press moves fast and is sometimes opinionated, so for anything that affects your license (rules, settlements), confirm with a primary source: NAR, your MLS, your state regulator, or the court record. Links below are outlets I saw in search results; I did not subscribe to or fully review them. Some paid or login-gated content exists.

## News outlets
| Source | URL | Why follow |
|---|---|---|
| Inman | https://www.inman.com | Daily agent-focused news and opinion; strong on brokerage and portal stories. |
| HousingWire | https://www.housingwire.com | Mortgage and real estate business news; good on rates, lawsuits, and tech. |
| Real Estate News | https://www.realestatenews.com | Detailed legal and brokerage coverage, including court cases (commission suits, DOJ filings). |
| RISMedia | https://www.rismedia.com | Industry news and market data for brokers and agents. |
| The Real Deal | https://therealdeal.com | Big-brokerage deals and major-metro news. |
| NAR (news and Magazine) | https://www.nar.realtor/news | Primary source for NAR policy, legal summaries and market data. |
| Chicago Agent Magazine / Seattle Agent Magazine | https://chicagoagentmagazine.com ; https://seattleagentmagazine.com | Regional coverage with national stories (appear in 2025-26 results). |
| Online Marketplaces | https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com | Covers portals (Zillow, Realtor.com, etc.). |

## Compliance and regulators
- Your state real estate commission (see [[Advertising Rules by State]] for the main ones).
- Your MLS's rules updates (for example OneKey MLS posts "Policy Updates": https://corporate.onekeymls.com/about/updates ).
- Federal Register for HUD/FCC/FTC notices: https://www.federalregister.gov
- FTC business guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance

## How to use the news safely
- Put the date next to any rule you note. Rules and policies from 2024-25 have already changed (see [[Market & Industry Timeline 2024-2026]]).
- Follow primary sources for rules: NAR policy pages, MLS rulebooks, state commission sites.
- Don't repost a news rumor to clients as fact.
- For content ideas from news, see [[Social Media Playbook]].

## What to do
- [ ] Subscribe to two trade newsletters (for example Inman and Real Estate News).
- [ ] Bookmark your MLS rules page and your state commission's news page.
- [ ] Set a 15-minute weekly reading block.
- [ ] Check Freddie Mac's rate survey each Thursday before talking rates with clients.
- [ ] Ask your broker which podcasts/coaches they trust.
