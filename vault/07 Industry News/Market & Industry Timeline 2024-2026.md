# Market & Industry Timeline 2024-2026

**In short:** The big story of these years is the commission settlement and what followed: written buyer agreements, new MLS rules, portal-vs-brokerage fights, and giant brokerage deals. Mortgage rates swung between roughly 6% and 7.4%, and home sales stayed near 30-year lows. Dates below are from the linked sources; most were read via search summaries (site fetches were blocked), so treat details as "check the link" and re-verify before quoting publicly.
