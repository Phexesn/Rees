# Best Free Videos & Courses

**In short:** This is a curated starting list of free YouTube videos and free courses on phone photography, listing video, Reels, listing descriptions, open houses and agent safety. Every link below appeared in a search result during research on 2026-11-03. **I could not open YouTube**, so I have not watched these. The "what you'll learn" lines come from each result's title and description snippet, not from the video. Creator names, lengths and upload dates were often not shown in results; where they were not, the table says so rather than guessing. Check upload date and view count before relying on any video, because phone menus and app interfaces change.

> How to use this list: pick one video per topic, watch at 1.25x, and apply it to a real listing the same week. Match what you learn against [[Phone Photography Guide]], [[Listing Video & Reels Guide]] and [[Open House Playbook]], and ignore any advice that conflicts with your MLS rules or [[Fair Housing Advertising]].

Caveats seen in results: several photography videos promote an app or accessory (a "SnapSnapSnap" app and a fisheye lens/correction feature were mentioned), so treat them as partly sponsored. Some results were several years old.
