# Free Tools List

**In short:** Most of what a new agent needs for photos, video and social posts is free or cheap: Snapseed for photo fixes, CapCut for video editing, Canva for graphics and flyers, Lightroom Mobile for basic edits, and a Google Business Profile for local search. Free tiers change often, so check what is locked before you build a habit around a feature. This list only includes tools I saw in search results; prices are what sources reported and can be out of date.

> Verification status: tool descriptions come from search summaries and vendor pages. I could not open most official sites. Prices and free-tier limits are marked where sources disagree.

## Paid options seen in results (for reference)
- CapCut Pro (price varies by source).
- Lightroom Premium (price not checked).
- Template bundles on Gumroad, Etsy, Creative Market (for example a Gumroad pack listed at $19 and a Creative Market kit with 120+ Instagram post templates). Not needed to start.

## Setup checklist
- [ ] Install Snapseed and CapCut; log in once to check what is free.
- [ ] Make a Canva account; save a "Just Listed" and "Open House" template with your brand colors.
- [ ] Claim or create your Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Switch social accounts to business/professional accounts.
- [ ] Save your brokerage name, license number and logo in one folder for quick use.
