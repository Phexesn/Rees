# Open House Follow-Up

**In short:** Visitors who sign in are warm contacts, but a sign-in sheet is **not automatic permission** to text or call with marketing. Follow up quickly, be useful, and respect the consent the visitor actually gave. All wording below is original example language; have your broker approve it.

## Consent reminders
- **Calls and texts:** Marketing calls or texts to a mobile number can require prior express written consent under the TCPA. A sign-in sheet that doesn't clearly ask for consent may not be enough. Ask your broker. See [[Texting & Calling Leads (TCPA)]] and use the optional consent language in [[Template - Open House Sign-In]].
- **Do Not Call:** If a visitor is on the National Do Not Call Registry, a visit and inquiry may create a limited window for contact. Trade summaries mention periods such as three months after an inquiry and 18 months after a transaction; **verify with your broker** (from search summary of [Florida Realtors](https://floridarealtors.org/law-ethics/library/do-not-call)).
- **Email:** Include your identity, physical address and an unsubscribe option. See [[Email Marketing Rules (CAN-SPAM)]].
- **Honor opt-outs immediately.**
- **Visitors who have an agent:** Don't try to pull them away from a signed agreement. Say thanks and offer help with the property info.
- **Sign-in is voluntary.** Don't require ID or contact details to enter; this may be addressed by your broker's and seller's policies (and safety). Explain how info will be used.
- **Fair Housing:** Treat all visitors the same way. See [[Fair Housing Advertising]].
- **Buyer agreements:** If a visitor wants to tour with you beyond the open house, you'll need a written agreement. Ask your broker how the rules apply to open houses; see [[Buyer Consultation Guide]].

## Timeline
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Same day (evening) | Email: thank-you with property facts and link |
| Next day | Call (where consent/lawful) |
| Day 3 | Email: similar homes |
| Day 7 | Call or text (with consent): any questions? |
| Day 14+ | Move to [[Follow-Up Cadence]] nurture |

## Scripts (examples)

### Call to a visitor
"Hi [Name], this is [You] with [Brokerage]. You visited [address] on [day]. Thanks for coming. I wanted to ask what you thought. Did anything stand out?"
- Then ask: "What are you looking for in a home?" "What's your timeline?" "Are you working with an agent already?"
- If yes: "Great. I'm glad you have help. Let me know if you have questions about [address]."
- If no: "Would it be useful if I sent a few similar homes?"

### Voicemail
"Hi [Name], [You] from [Brokerage]. Thanks for stopping by [address]. If you have questions or would like to see other homes, call or text [number]."

### Text (only with consent)
"Hi [Name], it's [You] from [Brokerage]. Thanks for visiting [address] today. Any questions I can answer? Reply STOP to opt out."

### Email
Subject: Thanks for visiting [address]
"Hi [Name], thank you for coming to the open house at [address]. Here are the details: [price, lot, a link]. If you'd like to see similar homes or have questions about the neighborhood, reply here or call [number]. [Signature, brokerage, license no., address, unsubscribe link]"

### Handwritten note
"[Name], thanks for stopping by [address] today. If I can help with your search, I'm at [number]. [You]."

### Script for sellers (after the event)
"Hi [Seller], here's the summary: [X] groups visited, comments included [common feedback], and next steps are [...]." (Share feedback accurately and neutrally, including negative comments.)

## What to avoid
- Adding visitors to an email list without telling them.
- Texting every number on the sheet by default.
- Pressure to sign anything on the spot.
- Describing neighbors or "who lives here." See [[Open House Playbook]] for the event itself.
