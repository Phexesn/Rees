# Gear Under $100

**In short:** You need very little. A phone tripod and a small light make the biggest difference for photos; a budget gimbal makes walkthrough video smoother. Most current phones already have an ultra-wide lens, so skip clip-on lenses unless your phone is old. **Prices change constantly and I could not see live retailer pages**, so every price below is a snapshot from the dated source shown. Price as of 2026-11: not confirmed for any item; re-check before buying.

## 4. Clip-on phone lenses

- Most phones from the last several years already have a 0.5x ultra-wide camera; reviewers (Digital Camera World) note the iPhone's native 0.5x is wider than the Apexel 0.45x clip-on, so clip-ons help mostly older phones.
- Apexel 2-in-1 wide plus macro: $26.99 per Digital Camera World's review; some users report vignetting and trouble with cases.
- Moment 18mm wide lens: about $150 per a 2026 roundup and needs a compatible case, so it is above this budget (unverified price).
- Verdict: skip unless your phone has no ultra-wide camera.

## 5. Other cheap helpers

- Microfiber cloth for the lens ($5 range, common practice)
- Bluetooth shutter remote (price not verified)
- Spare battery bank (price not verified)
- Small step ladder or stool for high exterior shots (common practice)
- Lens cleaning wipes
