# Social Media Playbook

**In short:** For a listing, pick two or three platforms and post a simple sequence: coming soon or teaser, just listed, open house, a price or status update, then just sold. Use real photos and short video, write captions that lead with a concrete fact, keep hashtags to five or fewer on Instagram, and follow your MLS, broker and Fair Housing rules ([[Fair Housing Advertising]]). Consistency beats volume.

> Verification status: Platform limits come from marketing blogs and news coverage because the platforms' own help pages were not reachable. Posting-frequency advice comes from real estate marketing blogs that disagree with each other; use it as a starting point, not a rule.

## 1. Platform limits (as of 2026-11-03)

| Platform | Key limits and rules | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | **Hashtags: up to 5 per post or Reel.** Announced 18-19 Dec 2025 via the @creators account, replacing the old limit of 30 (Later, Greenhouse Management, Jenn's Trends). Later says comments count too. **Caption: 2,200 characters**, with only the first ~125 characters visible before "more" (Outfy, Bitly, NapoleonCat). Links in captions are not clickable. | Hashtag cap and caption limit: several marketing blogs agree; I could not open Instagram's own post or Help Center |
| Facebook | No hard post limit found. Since December 2022 real estate listing content reportedly must go through a personal profile rather than a business Page (Inman, Jan 2023); that policy may have changed. | Secondary, possibly stale |
| TikTok | Caption limit reported as 2,200 (API) or 4,000 (in-app); sources conflict. Use short captions anyway. | Conflicting |
| YouTube Shorts | Up to 3 minutes since October 2024. | PetaPixel via earlier research draft |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters per post; about 140 characters show before "see more" on desktop. | Several vendor guides |
| X (Twitter) | 280 characters for free accounts; longer posts for Premium. | Vendor guides, earlier research draft |

**Reminders**
- No contact info or open house times in **MLS** remarks, but they are fine on social, subject to your broker's advertising policy.
- Include your brokerage name and any required license information. A missing brokerage name can make an ad a "blind ad" in some states (Berkshire Realtors article, state-dependent).
- Do not post another brokerage's listing without permission.
- Do not post private client information; get written permission for client photos and testimonials.

## 2. What to post per listing stage

| Stage | Instagram | Facebook | TikTok / Shorts | LinkedIn | X |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Coming soon** (if allowed by your MLS) | 1 teaser Reel or story | Short post with one photo | Teaser clip | Optional | Optional |
| **Just listed** | Carousel of 8-10 photos + 20-30 sec Reel | Post with 5-10 photos and a link | Vertical tour | Post if it has a business angle (investor, relocation) | One photo + link |
| **Open house** | Story with time and location; Reel the day of | Facebook event + post | "Open house this weekend" clip | Rarely | Short post |
| **Price improvement** | Story or single-photo post stating the new price | Post with the new price | Optional | Optional | Short post |
| **Under contract / sold** | "Just sold" photo (ask clients first) | Post with a thank-you | Optional | Post with a lesson or market note | Optional |

Caption pattern (Instagram, Facebook):
1. **Line 1:** concrete hook with price or standout feature (the first ~125 characters show before "more" on Instagram).
2. **2-4 short lines:** features, neighborhood fact, one reason to look.
3. **One call to action:** "DM for details", "Message me for a showing".
4. **Up to 5 hashtags** on Instagram (location, property type, niche).
5. **Brokerage name and any required license line.**

Do not use phrases that signal preference for a group of people; use feature-based wording ([[Listing Description Guide]]).

## 3. Platform notes

- **Instagram:** Reels get discovery; carousels hold details. Alt text on images should describe the room.
- **Facebook:** Good for neighbors and local groups. Read each group's rules; many ban listing posts. Lead with value (market info, neighborhood tips) before listings.
- **TikTok and YouTube Shorts:** Reuse the same vertical file ([[Listing Video & Reels Guide]]); use business-licensed music.
- **LinkedIn:** Better for market commentary, relocation and investment angles than for single listings. Avoid guaranteed-return language.
- **X:** Low priority for most agents; one post per milestone is plenty.
- **Google Business Profile** (not social, but free and local): see [[Free Tools List]].

## 4. Posting frequency (starting points)

Sources (marketing blogs) disagree. Treat these as ranges to test:

| Platform | Range cited | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 1-2 posts per day (attributed to Hootsuite in a Carrot article); another analysis claims 2-4 per week performs best for real estate | Carrot blog; PropData/other, second-hand |
| Instagram | "As often as you can post quality content"; one analysis suggests 2-6 times per week | Coffee Contracts blog |
| TikTok | 3-5 posts per week sustainable; one source says daily | Real estate marketing blogs |
| General | 3-5 posts per week across platforms; consistency matters more than volume | Keeping Current Matters, Luxury Presence |

**A realistic beginner plan:** 3 posts per week on one main platform plus the same video on a second. Add stories on open house days.

## 5. Two-week listing launch calendar (my design)

This is a plan, not a researched formula. Adjust to your MLS "coming soon" rules and your seller's agreement. Day 1 = the day you plan to go live (or the day you start teasing).

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| **Day -3** | Photos edited, video shot, captions written, check Fair Housing and MLS compliance. Get seller's written OK to post. |
| **Day -1** | Teaser story or short Reel if allowed ("New listing coming"). Create open house graphic in Canva ([[Free Tools List]]). |
| **Day 1** | List on MLS. Post carousel + vertical Reel on Instagram; Facebook post with link; TikTok/Shorts same video. Pin the post. |
| **Day 2** | Share to stories; send to your email list and text list where you have consent ([[Lead Follow-Up Scripts]]). |
| **Day 3** | Neighborhood content post (a nearby place, with the listing in the corner). |
| **Day 4** | Post a short "feature spotlight" (kitchen close-up). |
| **Day 5** | Open house promo: Facebook event, Instagram story with address area and time. |
| **Day 6** | Open house day: live stories, Reel. See [[Open House Playbook]]. |
| **Day 7** | Thank-you post with turnout (no names) and a call to action for private showings. |
| **Day 8** | Behind-the-scenes or "what I'd tell a buyer about this street". |
| **Day 9** | Market-data post tied to the listing (see [[CMA Basics]] for pulling facts). |
| **Day 10** | Second Reel with a different hook (outdoor space, a new detail). |
| **Day 11** | Reply to every comment and DM; send follow-up messages ([[Open House Follow-Up]]). |
| **Day 12** | Price improvement or status update post if the strategy calls for it. |
| **Day 13** | Re-share top performing post as a story; ask for reposts from your broker. |
| **Day 14** | Review: saves, shares, DMs, showings. Decide the next two weeks. |

## 6. Compliance checklist before you hit post

- [ ] Seller approved the post
- [ ] Brokerage name included
- [ ] No Fair Housing risk wording
- [ ] No private info (key location, lockbox code, alarm code, seller schedule)
- [ ] Photo and edit disclosures made
- [ ] Music licensed for business use
- [ ] Not another broker's listing without permission
- [ ] Hashtags 5 or fewer on Instagram
