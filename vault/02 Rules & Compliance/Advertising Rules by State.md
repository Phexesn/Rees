# Advertising Rules by State (10 biggest markets)

**In short:** Every state requires real estate ads to show who you work for, but the details differ: some states require your license number, some forbid advertising under your own name alone, some regulate team names, and most treat social posts and websites as advertising. This note summarizes the core rule in CA, TX, FL, NY, IL, GA, NC, AZ, WA and NJ with links to the official or closest-to-official text. These are summaries from search results, not the full rules. Every entry is marked "verify" for you to read the live rule before you print a card or post a sign.

## Quick comparison

| State | Brokerage name in ads? | License number in ads? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA | Designation required (licensed activity) | Required on first-point-of-contact solicitations; DRE chart lists license numbers for print ads (verify) | Also B&P 10140.8 on altered images (see [[AI in Real Estate - Rules & Risks]]) |
| TX | Yes: broker name; size rules | Not required by TREC rule (statute limits TREC) | Team and assumed names must be registered |
| FL | Yes: licensed brokerage name (+ phone, verify) | Not covered in sources found: verify | Individuals can't advertise only under their own name; team ads have own rule |
| NY | Yes: brokerage name or logo | Not covered in sources found: verify | Team names must include "team" |
| IL | Yes: sponsoring broker's business name | Not covered in sources found: verify | Broker name at least as large as team/individual name |
| GA | Yes: firm name registered with GREC | Not covered in sources found: verify | Firm name and phone at least as prominent as licensee's |
| NC | Yes: firm or sole proprietorship name | Not covered in sources found: verify | Needs broker-in-charge consent; social posts count |
| AZ | Yes: employing broker's licensed name, clear and prominent | Not covered in sources found: verify | Rule revised effective Dec 13, 2025 |
| WA | Yes: firm name or licensed assumed name | Not covered in sources found: verify | Branding/assumed name rules |
| NJ | Yes: broker's regular business name on record | Not covered in sources found: verify | Rule is N.J.A.C. 11:5-6.1 |

## California (Department of Real Estate)
- B&P Code 10140.6 and Regulation 2770.1 require a designation (e.g., "broker", "agent") in ads showing you are doing licensed work. Source: DRE summary at https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/reb/rebsum_03.pdf and regulation text https://regulations.justia.com/states/california/title-10/chapter-6/article-9/section-2770-1
- DRE's advertising chart lists an 8-digit license number for each licensee named in print ads: https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re559.pdf . For "first point of contact" solicitations, a law-firm alert summarizing DRE's license-ID requirement and Reg. 2773: https://www.kts-law.com/license-number-disclosure-requirements-for-real-estate-agents-and-brokers/ and DRE notice https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/WhatsNew/FirstPointContactSolicitationsAmendedReg3-28-2019.pdf . Verify the exact current media covered (social, email, cards).
- Internet advertising rule: https://regulations.justia.com/states/california/title-10/chapter-6/article-9/section-2770 . DRE overview of RE 858: https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re858.pdf

## Texas (TREC)
- Rule 535.154 (names) and 535.155 (advertising content), effective May 15, 2018 per a TREC-related summary. TREC pages: https://www.trec.texas.gov/article/trecs-advertising-rules-what-you-need-know , https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/323 , https://www.trec.texas.gov/sites/default/files/pdf-forms/TREC%20Advertisement%20Rule%20Review.pdf
- Ads must show the license holder's or team's name and the broker's name, with the broker's name at least half the size of the largest contact info for any agent or team in the ad (from search summary).
- Assumed names and team names must be registered via the broker. Team names must end in "team" or "group" and not use words like "brokerage" or "company" (summary: verify).
- License numbers: TREC FAQ says license holders are not required to put a license number on a sign or other ad. That statement is from TREC's FAQ page above; confirm it is still current, because 2026 rule changes were mentioned in TREC news that I did not check.

## Florida (Real Estate Commission, DBPR)
- Rule 61J2-10.025: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-Ann-R-61J2-10-025 (also https://flrules.org). Ads must show the licensed brokerage name and phone (a Jacksonville Daily Record Q&A says phone isn't required in newspaper ads; verify). Sales associates cannot advertise only under their own name. For internet ads, the brokerage name sits adjacent to or directly above/below the contact info.
- Teams: Rule 61J2-10.026: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-Ann-R-61J2-10-026

## New York (Department of State)
- 19 NYCRR 175.25, comprehensive amendments effective Nov 2, 2020. DOS advertising guidance: https://www.dos.ny.gov/licensing/re_advertising/real%20estate%20advertising%20guidance.pdf and https://dos.ny.gov/text-17525vlmb6619
- Ads may name associated licensees or teams but must also show the brokerage's name or logo. Team names must include "team". Nicknames are allowed only if the full licensed name also appears. Rules apply on third-party platforms (from DOS guidance summary). Check the full text for business cards and first-contact emails.

## Illinois (IDFPR)
- Real Estate License Act, 225 ILCS 454/10-30: https://ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/documents/022504540K10-30.htm . Ads must be under the sponsoring broker's business name; no licensee may list their own name without the sponsoring broker's business name; the broker name must be at least equal in size to a team or individual name. Rule: 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1450.750 (https://www.ilga.gov/ftp/JCAR/AdminCode/068/068014500G07150R.html ). A 2025-26 bill (HB 3186) proposes amending 10-30; its status: unverified.

## Georgia (GREC)
- Rule 520-1-.09: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/georgia/Ga-Comp-R-Regs-R-520-1-.09 . All advertising must include the firm name as registered with GREC; a registered trade name can replace it. For specific properties, the firm name and phone appear in equal or greater size/prominence/frequency than any licensee's. GREC newsletter summary: https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_ggpd_i-ga-bs720-pr4-bp1-br4-b18-s10-belec-p-btext . Verify current text; GREC has discussed rule updates.

## North Carolina (NCREC)
- 21 NCAC 58A .0105: https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/regulation/north-carolina-administrative-code/title-21-occupational-licensing-boards-and-commissions/chapter-58-real-estate-commission/subchapter-a-real-estate-brokers/section-0100-general-brokerage/section-58a-0105-advertising . Ads for brokerage services must include the name of the firm or sole proprietorship, and need broker-in-charge consent. Social media posts count: https://www.ncrealtors.org/?p=55041 . A 2025 Commission bulletin mentions .0105 amendments, so confirm current text: https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/2025/11/

## Arizona (ADRE)
- A.A.C. R4-28-502 (subsection E: employing broker's legal or DBA name on its license must appear clearly and prominently): https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/arizona/Ariz-Admin-Code-SS-R4-28-502 ; ADRE advertising article: https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/Forms/Licensing/ADRE-AdvertisingArticle%2003082022%20%281%29.pdf ; revisions effective Dec 13, 2025 (AAR summary): https://www.aaronline.com/adre-rule-revisions-effective-december-13-2025 . A logo or trade name alone may not be enough. The designated broker supervises all advertising. Verify the current rule text.

## Washington (Department of Licensing)
- WAC 308-124B-210 (firm name or licensed assumed name in all advertising, incl. internet and email): https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/WACArchive/2024/pdf/WAC%20308%20-124B%20CHAPTER/WAC%20308%20-124B-210.pdf . DOL guidelines: https://dol.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/real-estate-advertising-guidelines.pdf . Team-name rules: not verified, ask DOL/your broker.

## New Jersey (Real Estate Commission)
- N.J.A.C. 11:5-6.1 (note: 11:5-6.3 is not the advertising rule): https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-11-5-6-1 . Ads must include the broker's regular business name on record; salespersons add their own licensed name plus the broker's name; an ad may not present a location as an office unless it is the licensed main or branch office. Verify current text.

## Rules that apply everywhere
- Truthful ads and no "bait" claims. Fair housing: [[Fair Housing Advertising]].
- Texting/calling: [[Texting & Calling Leads (TCPA)]]. Email: [[Email Marketing Rules (CAN-SPAM)]].
- Social posts and bios often count as advertising: [[Social Media Playbook]].
- Photos you take for a listing: [[Phone Photography Guide]].

## What to do
- [ ] Open your state's rule (links above) and print the one-page requirements for your desk.
- [ ] Put your brokerage name where required on cards, signs, websites, social bios, emails.
- [ ] Check whether your state requires your license number (CA does in some cases).
- [ ] If you're on a team, check team-name rules and register the name through your broker.
- [ ] Get your broker's sign-off on templates before publishing.
- [ ] Re-check each January and after your state's rule notices.
