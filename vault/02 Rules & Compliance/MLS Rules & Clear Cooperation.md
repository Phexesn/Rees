# MLS Rules & Clear Cooperation

**In short:** NAR's Clear Cooperation Policy (CCP) says that once you publicly market a listing, you must put it in the MLS within one business day. In March 2025 NAR kept CCP but added a "delayed marketing exempt" option so sellers can keep a listing off public sites for a period set by each MLS, if they sign a disclosure. Zillow then added its own Listing Access Standards that go further than NAR. Always read your local MLS rulebook, because local rules and fines differ.

## Clear Cooperation Policy basics
- Requirement: file the listing with the MLS within one business day of public marketing (NAR policy page: https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/policies/mls-clear-cooperation-policy ).
- "Public marketing" is broad: yard signs, social posts, flyers, internet ads, brokerage website displays. Check your MLS for its exact definition.
- Enforcement is local: each MLS sets fines and process.

## NAR's 2025 change: Multiple Listing Options for Sellers
- Announced March 25, 2025; MLSs had to implement by September 30, 2025. Sources: https://www.nar.realtor/press-releases/nar-introduces-new-mls-policy-to-expand-choice-for-consumers , https://www.housingwire.com/articles/nar-unveils-new-mls-options-policy-with-delayed-marketing/ , https://therealdeal.com/national/2025/03/25/nar-to-keep-clear-cooperation-policy-with-a-twist
- Adds a "delayed marketing exempt" listing category. Those listings are filed with the MLS and visible to MLS participants, but the MLS and participants are delayed from showing them via IDX feeds and syndicating them (NAR news page: https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/nar-introduces-new-flexibility-for-sellers-while-retaining-clear-cooperation-policy ).
- Each MLS decides how long the delay can be. NAR does not set it.
- The seller must sign a disclosure acknowledging they give up the benefits of immediate public marketing. For office exclusives, a seller certification is also required (same NAR page).
- The one-business-day CCP requirement and local filing deadlines are unchanged.
- Critics argued it weakens CCP. Inman column: https://news.inman.com/2025/03/28/the-ccp-illusion-nars-jedi-mind-trick-on-a-failing-policy/ . One claim in that coverage (days-on-market and price-history display becoming optional locally) is unverified.
- Adoption varies by MLS. Read your MLS's version (examples: https://wmlar.com/clear-cooperation-change/ , https://berkshirerealtors.net/clear-cooperation-policy-change-multiple-listing-options-for-sellers/ ).

## Zillow's Listing Access Standards (LAS)
- Announced April 10, 2025 (Inman: https://www.inman.com/2025/04/10/zillow-to-prohibit-listings-that-are-privately-marketed/ ). Notices began May 28; blocking began June 30, 2025 (https://www.housingwire.com/articles/zillow-ban-noncompliant-listings-june-30-clear-cooperation-office-exclusives/ , https://www.inman.com/2025/06/30/its-reckoning-day-for-private-listings-what-you-should-know-about-zillows-changes/ ).
- Original design: listings publicly marketed but not on the MLS within 24 hours could be blocked from Zillow and Trulia. Per Inman coverage, the ban hit an agent's third non-compliant listing and after, and blocked listings stayed off the sites for the life of the listing agreement. FSBO, rentals and builder-direct new construction were out of scope (https://seattleagentmagazine.com/2025/05/22/zillow-listing-access-standards/ ).
- A seller could keep a listing fully private with an informed written agreement not to distribute it online.

## Compass vs. Zillow
- Compass sued Zillow over the standards (filing date: unverified — check the court docket; case in S.D.N.Y.).
- Feb 2026: Judge Jeannette Vargas denied Compass's preliminary injunction, finding Compass had not shown Zillow monopoly power and rejecting the Redfin-boycott theory. Not a final ruling on the merits (https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/02/06/zillow-can-continue-enforcing-private-listing-ban-judge-rules ; NAR summary: https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/law-and-ethics/judge-rejects-compasss-request-to-block-zillows-private-listing-rule ).
- March 17-18, 2026: Zillow revised its standards (no 24-hour timeline, no requirement to appear on Zillow, but listings in private networks marketed to consumers remain barred), launched "Zillow Preview" (showing listings before MLS entry, with partners including Keller Williams and RE/MAX), and Compass dismissed its suit without prejudice, meaning it could refile (https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/03/18/compass-drops-lawsuit-after-zillow-embraces-pre-marketing , https://www.housingwire.com/articles/compass-dismisses-zillow-lawsuit/ , Zillow's own statement: https://www.zillow.com/news/compass-abandons-lawsuit-affirming-zillow-pro-consumer-listing-standards/ ).
- Zillow and Compass describe the outcome differently. Check Zillow's current published standards before relying on any of this. Changes after March 2026: (unverified).

## What agents should know
- A "coming soon" or private marketing play can break MLS rules, Zillow rules, or both. They are separate rule sets.
- If a seller wants privacy, document the choice: a written disclosure of tradeoffs (fewer eyes on the listing, less online exposure).
- Your brokerage's policy may be stricter than the MLS. Ask first.
- Office exclusives and compensation language interact with [[NAR Settlement Changes]]: no compensation offers in the MLS.
- Ads must still carry required brokerage identification: [[Advertising Rules by State]].

## What to do
- [ ] Read your MLS's CCP, delayed-marketing and fine schedule.
- [ ] Before any public marketing (signs, posts, flyers), confirm the listing is in the MLS or properly exempt.
- [ ] Get the signed seller disclosure for delayed marketing or office exclusive.
- [ ] Check Zillow's current Listing Access Standards if you market listings off-MLS.
- [ ] Keep a dated record of when marketing started and when the MLS entry was made.
- [ ] Ask your broker which "pre-marketing" tools they allow.
